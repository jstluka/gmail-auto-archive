# gmail-auto-archive
These scripts auto-archive emails after a given length of time in an inbox.

I created the scripts here in conjunction with some Gmail filters to declutter a shared work email inbox.

Various notifications that are only relevant for a few hours can be auto-archived after six hours (i.e., "... has joined your Zoom room", "... has declined an invite", etc.), while emails pertaining to monthly bills should not be archived until later in the month. These scripts also operate on the assumption that emails older than 100 days can all be archived.

I also created the unstarOldEmails script so that my team members and I can star emails that should be brought to the attention of our CEO, and automatically removes the star at a time when this email is almost certainly no longer relevant.
